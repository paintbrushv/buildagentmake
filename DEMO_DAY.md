## Presentation plan

Open with: **“I’m building an inspection drone. Who can make this part?”**

Follow the buyer from a part requirement to a shortlist, distinguish an old certificate copy from a confirmed certification lapse, request the missing evidence, record a named review, and prepare the quote request. Then show how a drawing change requires fresh approval and how another American shop can add its capabilities and become discoverable.

Use the completed video for the full 8:46 walkthrough or the live app for selected steps and questions. Close on helping more American ideas reach American factory floors, with agent-to-agent coordination as the next development direction. Connected agents, real supplier messaging and independently authenticated qualifications remain future work.
