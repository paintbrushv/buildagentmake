# Forgepath — Supplier Desk

**Track:** Supplier Relationship Management

**Tagline:** Make domestic manufacturing capability discoverable, then establish a traceable next step.

## Inspiration

A regional manufacturer may have the right machines and know-how while remaining difficult for an unfamiliar buyer to discover. A supplier list also leaves the buyer with unanswered questions about process fit, material, capacity and evidence. Forgepath demonstrates a short path from a component requirement to a named human decision and a useful RFQ review packet.

## What it does

Click a part on a conceptual commercial inspection drone, compare suppliers against explicit requirements, open an evidence follow-up, review a new synthetic qualification packet, record human approval, and download a readable HTML packet plus structured JSON. Changed requirements, capability records, evidence or qualification expiry invalidate earlier approvals.

A manufacturer can paste a short capability card, review the parsed fields and become searchable immediately in the demo workspace. Common aliases normalize; arbitrary processes and materials are accepted. Discovery does not require completed qualification. Qualification remains a separate accountable review.

## How it works

Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript with no third-party packages, credentials or external APIs. Deterministic scoring explains five dimensions, while every required capability/qualification/site check gates approval. All state stays in the visitor's browser. Packets snapshot the requirement revision, supplier facts, comparisons, evidence provenance, reviewer, decision and next steps.

## Evidence and limits

All scored manufacturers, part specifications, orders and qualification evidence are fictional. Official Muenster machining capability references are separate research context. No claim is made about real supplier participation, drone part production, current capacity, certification, adoption, airworthiness or domestic content of every input. Human reviews record demo assertions; the app does not independently authenticate certificates. 32 tests pass and the main browser journey was exercised.
